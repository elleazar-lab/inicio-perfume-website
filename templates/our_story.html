Our Story

Inspired by boldness, perfected through performance

The Beginning

INICIO Parfum was born from a simple yet powerful idea: to create fragrances that capture the essence of bold individuality and timeless elegance. What started as a passion project in a small studio has grown into a brand that celebrates the art of fine perfumery.

Our journey began with a single bottle, crafted with care and precision, using only the finest ingredients sourced from around the world. Every scent tells a story, and we wanted our fragrances to speak to those who dare to be different.

Our Philosophy

We believe that fragrance is more than just a scent — it's an extension of your personality, a signature that lingers long after you've left the room. That's why each of our perfumes is carefully formulated to balance complexity with wearability.

From fresh and subtle notes to bold and captivating blends, our collection is designed to move with you through every moment of your day. We source sustainable ingredients and work with master perfumers to ensure every bottle meets our highest standards.

"Scent is our most primitive sense — it's the fastest hit to the limbic system, an even more direct route to this part of the brain than sight."

— Catherine Haley Epstein

Looking Forward

As we continue to grow, our commitment remains the same: to create fragrances that inspire confidence, spark joy, and leave a lasting impression. We're excited to share our journey with you and can't wait to see where your scent journey takes you.

Thank you for being part of the INICIO story. Here's to many more fragrant adventures together.
