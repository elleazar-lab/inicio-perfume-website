Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about INICIO Parfum

What is INICIO Parfum? +
INICIO Parfum is a luxury fragrance brand inspired by boldness and perfected through performance. We create high-quality, long-lasting scents inspired by designer fragrances at an accessible price point.
How long does the fragrance last? +
Our fragrances are extrait de parfum concentration, which typically lasts 6-8 hours on skin and even longer on clothes. Longevity may vary depending on skin type and environmental factors.
Do you offer free shipping? +
Yes! We offer free shipping in Metro Manila, Cavite, and Laguna for orders ₱1,300 and above. For provincial areas, shipping is free for orders ₱3,000 and above.
How can I track my order? +
Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number to track your order on our website or the courier's portal.
What is your return policy? +
Due to hygiene reasons, we do not accept returns on fragrance products. However, if you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact our customer service within 48 hours of delivery.
Are your products original? +
INICIO Parfum creates inspired fragrances that capture the essence of luxury designer scents. All our products are crafted in-house using high-quality ingredients and are not affiliated with the original brands.
How do I apply the fragrance? +
For best results, apply to pulse points such as wrists, behind the ears, and the base of the throat. Avoid rubbing the fragrance as it can break down the scent molecules.
Do you ship internationally? +
Currently, we only ship within the Philippines. We are working on expanding our shipping options internationally in the near future.
